Version: 0.2.0
Date: 2026-09-07
  Changes:
    - Moved prototype creation to data-final-fixes.lua so base game data-updates
      don't overwrite scaled speed/max_speed values.
    - Recipes made more expensive: added steel plates, processing units,
      and increased ingredient counts to match belt-tier costs.
    - Reduced tint intensity on tiered bot icons (alpha 0.45, was 1.0).
    - Added optional superior tier (3×) when Krastorio 2 is installed.
      Uses kr-imersite-crystal, unlocked by kr-logistic-5 tech.
    - Added dedicated technologies (fast/express/turbo/superior robotics)
      with chained prerequisites: previous bot tech + corresponding belt tech.
    - Energy efficiency improved per tier: 0.80/0.65/0.50/0.35.
    - Energy storage scaled 1.5×/2×/2.5×/3× per tier.

Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-09-04
  Features:
    - Fast, express, and turbo tiers for logistic and construction robots.
    - Each tier improves speed, cargo capacity, energy storage, energy efficiency, and health.
    - Recipes chain: each tier consumes the previous tier, mirroring the belt upgrade chain.
    - Fast bots unlocked by Logistics 2, express by Logistics 3, turbo by Turbo transport belt (Space Age).
